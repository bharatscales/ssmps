Academics

From playful pre-primary foundations through Class 8 — our highest standard

Programmes

Classes We Offer

The school offers education from Nursery up to 8th standard only. Structured progression with age-appropriate methods, continuous evaluation, and remedial classes where needed.

LevelClassesFocus areas
Pre-PrimaryNursery, LKG, UKGPhonics, numeracy, motor skills, storytelling, social habits
PrimaryClass 1 – 5English, Hindi, Mathematics, EVS, art, moral science
Middle (highest level)Class 6 – 8Science, Social Science, Sanskrit/optional language, computer basics — final year is 8th standard

Teaching Approach

Interactive Classrooms

Question-led lessons, group work, and use of charts and models to make concepts clear.

Regular Assessments

Unit tests, term exams, and progress reports shared with parents.

Remedial Support

Extra help for students who need reinforcement in language or mathematics.

Class 8 Readiness

Revision, assessments, and guidance so students complete 8th standard with confidence.

Co-Curricular Activities

  • Annual day, Independence & Republic Day celebrations
  • Sports meets and indoor games periods

  • Science exhibitions and quiz competitions
  • Computer practicals and digital safety awareness
  • Essay, debate, and handwriting improvement programmes
