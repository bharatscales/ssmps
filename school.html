Sangeeta Singh Memorial Public School

Under the Anand Singh Shaishik Avam Samaj Kalyan Datavya Trust, Sangeeta Singh Memorial Public School provides quality school education in Amethi, Uttar Pradesh. Established in 2007, it is part of the Babu Ranjeet Singh Group of Institutions, serving families across rural Uttar Pradesh with a focus on strong foundations, values, and holistic child development.

The school offers education from Nursery to Class VIII, with dedicated teachers, a caring campus environment, and activities that support academic growth and character building. It works alongside the trust’s college and other institutions to extend the group’s mission of educational excellence in the region.

  • Nursery to Class VIII
  • Values-Based & Holistic Learning
  • Serving Amethi & Surrounding Areas
  • Part of Trust Since 2007
