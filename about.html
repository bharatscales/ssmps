About Our Trust

Dedicated to education, social welfare, and community development in Amethi and beyond.

Registered under the Indian Trust Act 1882 (Govt. of India)

Who We Are

Anand Singh Shaishik Avam Samaj Kalyan Datavya Trust is a registered charitable trust based in Amethi, Uttar Pradesh. Inspired by the vision of serving society through learning and compassion, we work to uplift rural and underserved communities by making quality education accessible and supporting meaningful social welfare programmes.

Through our institutions and outreach, we aim to nurture young minds, strengthen families, and contribute to lasting community development — one student, one family, and one village at a time.

Our Mission

To empower communities through accessible education, charitable initiatives, and programmes that promote dignity, health, and opportunity for all.

Our Vision

A society where every child can learn with hope, every family receives support in times of need, and education becomes the foundation of lasting progress in rural India.

What We Do

Three Pillars of Our Work

  • Education

    Running and supporting schools and colleges — including pharmacy and school-level programmes — so students in Amethi receive quality, career-ready learning.

  • Social Welfare

    Helping families through charitable support, health awareness, and initiatives that address real needs in our neighbourhoods.

  • Community Development

    Building stronger villages through volunteer action, local partnerships, and programmes that encourage participation and shared responsibility.
