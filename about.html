About Our School

Nursery to 8th standard — quality education for every child in Amethi

Our story

Smt. Sangeeta Singh Memorial Public School

Founded in memory of Sangeeta Singh, the school stands as a tribute to her belief in learning and service. Today, SSMPS is a growing institution on NH931 at Anand Nagar, beside Ramleela Ground — a landmark families across Amethi trust.

We welcome students from Agahar, Kohra, Jangal Ramnagar, and neighbouring villages, offering affordable, quality schooling from Nursery through 8th standard — the highest class at our school.

Purpose

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a leading community school in Amethi where every student achieves academic success and grows into a responsible citizen.

Mission

To provide safe, inclusive, and stimulating education; to support teachers with training; and to partner with parents for each child’s holistic development.

Values

Integrity, respect, discipline, curiosity, and compassion — reflected in daily assembly, classroom conduct, and community outreach.

Leadership

Dedicated to Spreading Education

Our headmistress and faculty are actively engaged in raising awareness about schooling in the region — encouraging enrolment, regular attendance, and parent participation. Local coverage has highlighted this commitment to making education accessible in Amethi.

The administration maintains open doors for guardians: you are welcome to discuss progress, fees, or any concern in person or by phone.

  • Experienced teachers for pre-primary, primary, and middle classes (up to 8th standard)
  • Subject specialists for Classes 6–8 including science and mathematics
  • Support staff for computer lab, games, music, and front office
