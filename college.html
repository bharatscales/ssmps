Babu Ranjeet Singh Educational Institute (BRSEI)

Under the Anand Singh Shaishik Avam Samaj Kalyan Datavya Trust, BRSEI is a pharmacy and paramedical college at Ranjeet Nagar, Kherauna Bypass, Amethi, Uttar Pradesh. Founded in 2019, it focuses on excellence in education and commitment to healthcare for students across rural Uttar Pradesh.

The institute is PCI approved (Code: 3393), affiliated with BTE Lucknow (Code: 2140) and AKTU, and offers modern labs, a medicinal garden, Wi‑Fi campus, library, transport, and placement support. D.Pharma admissions are open for 2026–27; B.Pharma is coming soon.

  • PCI Approved · BTE & AKTU Affiliated
  • D.Pharma (2 Years) — Admissions Open
  • 5 Pharmacy Labs & Medicinal Garden
  • Placement & Industry Guidance
