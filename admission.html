Admission

Enrol your child for Nursery through Class 8 — limited seats per class

How to Apply

Follow these steps to secure a seat. For urgent queries, call 9792201779 or email ssmps01@gmail.com.

1

Visit or Call

Contact the school office or visit Anand Nagar, Ramleela Ground, NH931, Amethi to check seat availability. Admissions are open for Nursery through Class 8 only.

2

Collect / Submit Form

Fill the admission form with student and guardian details. You may use the online enquiry below; final admission is confirmed at the office.

3

Documents

Submit birth certificate, previous report card (if applicable), Aadhaar, passport-size photos, and transfer certificate for students joining from another school.

4

Interaction & Fee

Short interaction may be scheduled for certain classes. Pay fees as per the school fee structure and collect receipt.

Documents Checklist

  • Birth certificate (original + photocopy)
  • Aadhaar card of student and parent
  • Two recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous year mark sheet / report card
  • Transfer certificate (if joining mid-session or from another school)
  • Caste certificate (if applicable for concessions)

Upload PDF forms here when available: documents/ folder. Ask the office for the latest fee structure.
