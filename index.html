About the school

Rooted in Amethi, Committed to Excellence

Sangeeta Singh Memorial Public School serves families across Agahar, Kohra, and surrounding areas of Amethi (District Sultanpur). Our campus on NH931 is easy to reach and well known in the locality.

Under dedicated leadership, the school works to spread awareness about the importance of education — helping children from diverse backgrounds learn with dignity and joy.
